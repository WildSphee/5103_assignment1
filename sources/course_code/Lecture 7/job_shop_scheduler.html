Job-shop scheduler

Define each job's routing, then book the time slots by hand and read off the schedule's performance.

1. Routing data

Leave a machine blank or a time at 0 to skip that step. Steps run left to right.

2. Book the schedule

Click a step above to select it, then click a time slot on its row. Selected block: arrow keys nudge it, Delete removes it, double-click also removes it. Conflicts are allowed but flagged in red.

3. Results

Instructor tools: solve for the optimal schedule

Minimizes total completion time (equivalently, total waiting time) with an exhaustive branch-and-bound over active schedules. It proved optimality in under a second on random cases up to 8 rows × 5 steps. If a larger case hits the 5-second limit, it reports the best schedule found and says it is not proven optimal.
