Fashion Markdown

Season pricing simulator

You start the season with 2,000 units of a single item and 15 weeks to sell it. Week 1 is always priced at $60. From then on you may mark the price down — never back up — to $54, $48, or $36. Anything left at the end of week 15 is sold to a discounter for $25/unit. Set the price each week and see what happens.
