Geographic Distribution

Overview

Geospatial statistics are published by the Singapore Department of Statistics (DOS) and provide valuable insights into the geographical distribution and characteristics of Singapore's population. These statistics offer a comprehensive view of how residents are distributed across the island, their demographic profiles by location, and various socio-economic indicators at the geographical level.
