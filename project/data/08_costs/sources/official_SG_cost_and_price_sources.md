# Official Singapore sources: charger prices, tariffs, grants, hubs

Retrieved 2026-10-31. Items 1, 3 and 4 were extracted from client-rendered pages with a fetch tool (verbatim quotes). Items 2 and 5 are saved as raw text or PDF in this folder.

## 1. MOT written reply: EV charging rates at HDB and private carparks (22 Sep 2025)
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/written-reply-to-parliamentary-question-on-electric-vehicle-charging-rates-at-hdb-and-private-car-parks--and-impact-of-mandating-dual-operators/
- MP: Mr Low Wu Yang Andre | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- "Charging prices vary depending on operator, location and charger power rating. The median electric vehicle (EV) charging price is $0.66/kWh for Housing Development Board (HDB) carparks and $0.74/kWh for private commercial carparks. Charging prices are commercial decisions."
- "Tenders were evaluated on a price-quality framework where we weight quality more than price. Operators do not have exclusive rights to operate in HDB carparks; the Land Transport Authority may call a tender to install more EV chargers in future."

## 2. LTA charger grants (see `LTA_charger_grants_page.txt`, `LTA_ECCG_Guidelines_and_FAQs.pdf`)
- **ECCG** (shared chargers in condos/NLPRs): 50% of each cost component (charging system, LEW fees, cabling and installation with a S$1,000 sub-cap). Overall cap is S$4,000 per charger for the first 2,000 chargers and S$3,000 for the next 1,500. Funds up to 1% of carpark lots. Runs until 31 Dec 2026.
- **EHVCG** (electric heavy-vehicle chargers): "co-fund up to 50% of charger installation cost, capped at $30,000 per charger". Runs 1 Jan 2026 – 31 Dec 2028, first 500 chargers, max 3 per site.
- No grant exists for HDB public chargers. Those are installed and run by operators under LTA concession tenders, and operators pay LTA a concession fee (LTA news release, 2 Nov 2022).

## 3. SP Group: first HDB fast-charging hub, Tengah (16 May 2026)
- URL: https://www.spgroup.com.sg/about-us/media-resources/news-and-media-releases/SP-Group-launches-Singapore-first-EV-fast-charging-hub-in-a-HDB-carpark
- "eight direct current (DC) fast charging points of up to 100 kilowatts (kW) and six alternating current (AC) charging points at 7.4 kW each." Located at the carpark serving Blks 123 & 129 beside Plantation Plaza.
- More hubs from early 2027: Bedok North Ave 3, Teck Whye Lane, Pipit Road (MacPherson), Jurong East St 31, Tampines St 34, Woodlands St 13, Bukit Canberra, Jurong West Sports Centre.
- No cost or investment figure was disclosed.

## 4. EVe fast-charging hub contracts
- 13 Mar 2026: first contracts, 6 HDB sites, awarded to SP Mobility and Shell. First site operational early 2027. URL: https://eve.com.sg/news/eve-awards-first-contracts-to-deploy-fast-charging-hubs-in-hdb-towns
- 29 Apr 2026: second contracts, 8 sites (HDB and recreational-facility carparks), awarded to SP Mobility and ComfortDelGro ENGIE. URL: https://eve.com.sg/news/eve-awards-second-contracts-to-deploy-fast-charging-hubs-in-hdb-towns
- Goal: at least one fast-charging hub per HDB town by end-2027. No contract values were published.

## 5. SP Group electricity tariff, 1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026
- URL: https://www.spgroup.com.sg/our-services/utilities/tariff-information
- Household tariff: 28.59 ¢/kWh before GST (31.16 ¢/kWh incl. GST). Components: energy 22.18, network 6.10, MSS 0.23, market admin/PSO 0.08.
- Operators pay non-domestic tariffs, which differ. Use the household tariff only as a proxy for operator electricity cost.

## What is NOT published
- The capital cost per public charging point in Singapore (HDB tender TD116 concession fees appear only in an annex image, and hub contract values weren't released).
- The cost of electrical or substation upgrades at HDB carparks. LTA only says these are "extensive and costly". The 139 HDB carparks without chargers lack them because of insufficient power.
