## 1. Regular Publication of Data on EV Charger Utilisation Rates Across Public Carparks by Hour and Estate Demand
- Date: 7 May 2026 | MP: Ng Shi Xuan | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/regular-publication-of-data-on-electric-vehicle-charger-utilisation-rates-across-public-carparks-by-hour-and-estate-demand/

1. The average utilisation rate for EV chargers at the Housing and Development Board (HDB) carparks is about 20%, with higher utilisation typically at night. HDB chargers in Tengah, Punggol and Sengkang have higher utilisation rates. JTC-managed carparks have an average utilisation of about 9%, with higher utilisation in the day. The Singapore Land Authority has not deployed any charging points.
2. The Land Transport Authority publishes EV adoption and charger deployment data regularly, and will look into doing so for utilisation.

## 2. LTA Data Metrics to Determine EV Charging Point Adequacy in Bidadari Estate's HDB Carparks
- Date: 8 April 2026 | MP: Alex Yeo | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/lta-data-metrics-to-determine-ev-charging-point-adequacy-in-bidadari-estate-s-hdb-carparks/

1. LTA and its subsidiary, EV-Electric Charging Pte Ltd (EVe), monitor charger utilisation rates and work with EV Charging Operators (EVCOs) to scale up deployment in high demand areas, where electrical capacity permits.
2. In Bidadari Estate, the slow chargers are utilised for about 40% of the time on average, compared to about 20% nationwide. More slow charging points will be deployed in Bidadari this year. Electrical infrastructure upgrades are ongoing to enable the installation of a fast-charging hub in the future. In the meantime, there are already fast chargers available at Woodleigh Mall and nearby petrol kiosks.

## 3. Data on HDB Carparks Without EV Chargers and Access to Alternative Chargers
- Date: 5 May 2026 | MP: He Ting Ru | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/data-on-hdb-carparks-without-ev-chargers-and-access-to-alternative-chargers-/

1. As of March 2026, 139 HDB carparks (7% of the total) lack EV chargers due to technical constraints like insufficient power or community requests to delay installation. EV owners who use these carparks can transfer their season parking to nearby HDB carparks with EV chargers at the same season parking rate.
2. We have deployed around 30,500 charging points, and are on track to meet our target of 60,000 charging points by 2030. The public can track the rollout of EV chargers online through LTA's DataMall, which is updated every month.

## 4. Measures To Increase Charging Points In High Demand HDB Car Parks In View Of 60,000 EV Charging Points By 2030 Target
- Date: 3 February 2026 | MP: Ng Chee Meng | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/measures-to-increase-charging-points-in-high-demand-hdb-car-parks-in-view-of-60-000-ev-charging-points-by-2030-target/

1. We remain on track to achieve our target of deploying 60,000 charging points by 2030, in tandem with the growing demand for EVs.
2. HDB residents have access to EV chargers which are now installed in nearly every HDB carpark. LTA monitors their usage, and works closely with EV Charging Operators to install more charging points at carparks with high demand, where electrical capacity permits.

## 5. Statistics of EVs Registered for Season Parking in HDB Carparks and Increasing Charging Lots for Carparks with More EV Season Parkings
- Date: 11 November 2024 | MP: Dr Lim Wee Kiak | Reply: Minister for Transport Chee Hong Tat
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/written-reply-to-parliamentary-question-on-statistics-of-evs-registered-for-season-parking-in-hdb-carparks-and-increasing-charging-lots-for-carparks-with-more-ev-season-parkings-registered/

1. As of end September 2024, there are about 8,800 EVs registered with season parking at HDB carparks.
2. The average EV charger utilisation rate at HDB carparks is currently under 10%, and remains sufficient to meet the demand from EV drivers. We closely monitor EV charger utilisation at all HDB carparks, and work with the EV Charging Operators to scale up deployment of EV chargers where necessary.

## 6. Utilisation Patterns of EV Chargers at HDB Car Parks and Factors Determining Increased Deployment
- Date: 5 May 2026 | MP: Melvin Yong Yik Chye | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/utilisation-patterns-of-ev-chargers-at-hdb-car-parks-and-factors-determining-increased-deployment-of-such-chargers/

1. The average utilisation rate of EV chargers across all HDB carparks during the 3-hour peak of each carpark is 36% in February 2026. Tengah has the highest peak utilisation rate, at 61%. Utilisation and charging behaviour vary across carparks and with charger power ratings.
2. Besides utilisation and availability of electrical capacity, availability of parking lots and nearby charging options are also considered when determining installation plans.

## 7. Requirements For EV Charger Deployment In HDB Carparks and Utilisation Enforcement Measures
- Date: 25 September 2025 | MP: David Hoe | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/written-reply-to-parliamentary-question-on-requirements-for-ev-charger-deployment-in-hdb-carparks-and-utilisation-enforcement-measures/

1. LTA and EVe work closely with EVCOs, HDB and Town Councils to deploy EV chargers in HDB carparks. EV chargers are mostly sited on the third deck of multi-storey carparks to balance the needs of both EV and non-EV drivers and facilitate wayfinding.
2. Currently, the average charger utilisation rate of EV chargers at HDB carparks is around 10%, and remains sufficient to meet the demand from EV drivers. We are monitoring charger utilisation rates and will work with the EVCOs to scale up deployment in high demand areas, where electrical capacity permits. To deter lot hogging, EVCOs have implemented idle fees at some of their charging locations, especially for chargers with high demand.

## 8. Deployment of EV Charging Infrastructure in New Towns (oral reply)
- Date: 6 May 2026 | MP: Dr Choo Pei Ling | Reply: Senior Minister of State for Transport Sun Xueling
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/deployment-of-ev-charging-infrastructure-in-new-towns/

1. The Government targets to deploy 60,000 EV charging points by 2030, which comprise 40,000 in public car parks and 20,000 in private premises. As of March 2026, there are 30,500 EV charging points deployed in Singapore. We are on track to achieving our 2030 target in tandem with the growing demand for EVs.
2. LTA monitors EV adoption rates closely, and coordinates with agencies such as EMA, HDB and JTC to deploy EV chargers and key supporting infrastructure. For carparks with high demand, LTA will work with EV charger operators to deploy more chargers, electrical capacity permitting. By end 2027, there will also be at least one fast charging hub in every HDB town.

## 9. Ensuring Affordable Public EV Charging at HDB Carparks Through Energy Cost Monitoring and Safeguards
- Date: 5 May 2026 | MP: He Ting Ru | Reply: Acting Minister for Transport Jeffrey Siow
- URL: https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/ensuring-affordable-public-ev-charging-at-hdb-carparks-through-energy-cost-monitoring-and-safeguards/

1. While the Government does not intervene in pricing set by EVCOs, we ensure users have multiple options from a range of EVCOs, where feasible. For instance, TD116, the first large-scale tender for the supply of EV chargers in HDB carparks, selects two EVCOs to deploy and operate EV chargers in HDB carparks in each region. After the award of tenders, we expect EVCOs to be transparent in publishing prices as a means of keeping charging competitive.
