About this publication
This report presents the key findings on Singapore's population and households from the General Household Survey 2025, which provides a mid-decade update between Population Censuses. Broad trends and changes between 2020 and 2025 across topics comprising marriage and fertility, education, literacy and home language, religion, households and housing, household market income, married couples in resident households, geographic distribution, transport, and difficulty in performing basic activities are highlighted in the report. The report includes detailed statistical tables to enable more comprehensive study by users.
