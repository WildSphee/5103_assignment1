# Model references and boundaries of their use

1. **Church, R. and ReVelle, C. (1974), “The maximal covering location problem,” Papers of the Regional Science Association 32, 101–118.** [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1435-5597.1974.tb00902.x). Original covering-model reference: allocate limited facilities to maximise demand within a coverage distance. Bibliographic metadata was located; local full-text acquisition returned HTTP 403. Do not claim to have read or downloaded the full original. University-library retrieval remains an optional reference follow-up. Our finite-capacity energy flows, existing facilities and variable point counts are extensions, not the original binary MCLP formulation.
2. **Gurobi Optimization, facility.py example.** [Official example](https://docs.gurobi.com/projects/examples/en/current/examples/python/facility.html). Local `Gurobi_facility_example.html/.txt`. Demonstrates fixed opening costs, capacities, continuous transport assignments and binary open decisions. Our adaptation uses charger counts and grid upgrades, permits unmet demand, and treats distance as access rather than assigning an invented monetary travel charge.
3. **Gurobi Optimization, Multiple Objectives.** [Official reference](https://docs.gurobi.com/projects/optimizer/en/current/features/multiobjective.html). Local `Gurobi_multiple_objectives_2026-11-09.html/.txt`. Supports priorities and tolerance-aware sequential objectives. Our transparent implementation can instead perform two explicit solves and add a service lock. This is lexicographic deterministic optimisation, not stochastic recourse. Log MIP bounds and numerical tolerances.
4. **Brandstätter, G., Leitner, M. and Ljubić, I. (2018 manuscript), “Location of charging stations in electric car sharing systems.”** [Author-hosted manuscript](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/5933.pdf). Local PDF, dated 23 October 2018, previous version 2016. Relevant evidence that location, capacity, budgets and access interact. Their trip scheduling and battery-state model is a different application and is not adopted as an HDB residential demand model. Cite manuscript/version accurately unless publication metadata is independently checked.
5. **Course Project Guideline**, local `sources/references/Project_Guideline.pdf`, pp. 1–4. The hospital porter-station example explicitly combines facility location, transportation, MIP and sensitivity. Lecture 7 integer programming and Lecture 6 network materials are local teaching context, not substitutes for published model attribution.

Use the first three to explain the exact formulation; the fourth is related EV research, not a source of Singapore parameters. The model equations and constraints in `project/plan.md` are our proposed adaptation. Save final bibliographic formatting with the report, and distinguish accessed full text from metadata-only references.
