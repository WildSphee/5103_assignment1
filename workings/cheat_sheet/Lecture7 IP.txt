--- SLIDE 24 ---
Mathematical Definition (Symmetric TSP)
• Given a complete undirected graph and a cost 
on each arc, find a path starting and finishing 
at a specified node after having visited each 
other node exactly once (called Hamiltonian 
cycle) that minimizes the total cost.
• If in application, no arc exists between two 
nodes, we can assign an arbitrarily large cost 
on the arc connecting these two nodes.
